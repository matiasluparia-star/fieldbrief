# Nexotopo commercial preview

Entry: `/servicios.html`. The original assistant remains at `/index.html`.

Spanish landing page for a scoped intake/follow-up automation service across business sectors. Indicative implementation budget 300–500 EUR, excluding taxes and separately agreed third-party running costs. These are proposed commercial terms, not proven market pricing or confirmed customer outcomes.

## Working interactions

Browser-only fictional intake examples; editable message; rule-based categorisation; missing-information state; exact normalised duplicate detection; editable reply; status selection; JSON and spreadsheet-safe CSV export; reset. Software and document workflows are included. No Gmail, Google Sheets, external AI or background integrations are active in this demonstration. Nothing is sent automatically.

The enquiry form prepares a copyable/downloadable text brief. It is deliberately marked as a preview and never claims an enquiry was submitted. No contact information is collected or stored remotely. It must be connected to an owner-approved business contact channel before advertising or presenting it as lead capture. No private email or phone number was published.
